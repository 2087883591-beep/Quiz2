why reels are different

a reel is not a short vertical tiktok.

the same footage performs differently on reels because the ranking system rewards different behaviour. the analyzer reads a reel against reels mechanics, not generic short-form rules.

01

sends are the loudest signal.

a reel that gets forwarded travels further than one that only gets liked — so the analyzer looks for the sendable moment, not just the hook.

02

replays beat run time.

the breakdown maps where the edit closes back on itself and whether the last frame invites a second pass.

03

reels is its own ranking system.

feed, stories, explore and reels rank separately. early engagement velocity lets small accounts break out.

anatomy of a viral reel

five beats decide whether a reel travels.

0.0 – 1.0s

scroll stop

one visual with no setup, usually mid-motion. no logo, intro or slow pan.

1.0 – 3.0s

promise

states what the viewer gets if they stay — spoken, on-screen or visibly implied.

3.0 – 8.0s

escalation

cut rhythm tightens and every shot adds new information.

8s – end

payoff

the reveal, number or transformation timed against the full run length.

last 0.5s

loop close

the final frame matches the opening or hard-stops.
